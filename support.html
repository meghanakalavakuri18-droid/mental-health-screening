You Are Not Alone

Thank you for sharing your feelings. Your safety is important.

What you can do now

  • Contact someone you trust and tell them how you feel.
  • If possible, stay with a trusted person.
  • Move away from anything you might use to hurt yourself.
  • Seek help from a qualified mental health professional.

Emergency Services

Tele-MANAS

Call 14416 for mental health support.

If you are in immediate danger, do not wait for this website. Contact emergency services or go to the nearest hospital.
